Tell us about your project

From the challenge
to a clear deliverable.

Before starting, we define the objective, audience, existing assets, constraints and acceptance criteria. Scope and deliverables are agreed in the project brief. After delivery, we review data and feedback to inform the next decision.

Where do we start?

Describe your objective or challenge in the short form. If you’re unsure which service fits, request an introductory meeting so we can find the starting point together.

How are cost and timing determined?

Scope, deliverables, complexity, timing and each party’s responsibilities affect the estimate. Once the brief and requirements are clear, we prepare a tailored proposal.

Do we need every service within a Khan?

No. Services and expertise are chosen around the challenge. A project may focus on one service or combine several related ones.
