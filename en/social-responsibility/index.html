Social responsibility

Knowledge and creativity matter more when they create opportunity for people, local communities and the sea that sustains them.

Putting our skills to work for society

Three priorities for a positive contribution.

01

Access to practical skills

Practical marketing, media literacy and responsible AI skills for young people and small businesses, with particular attention to women and local talent seeking learning opportunities.

Explore our free learning resources
02

Opportunity for local communities

Specialist support for local businesses, creative enterprises and social organisations: clearer messaging, useful content and better audience connections.

03

Responsible marine & coastal practices

Communication campaigns and coastal events that respect local culture, livelihoods, safety and the marine environment. We work with local specialists on awareness and waste reduction.

Local insight comes first

Local needs. Shared progress.

Local needs come first. On Iran’s northern and southern coasts and in regional collaborations, we define the problem and response with host communities rather than applying one template everywhere.

Mataf contributes communication expertise. Environmental and safety assessments belong with qualified specialists and institutions.

How collaboration works

From a clear need to evidence of change.

01

Listen and understand

Clarify the need, location and audience with the local community.

02

Select responsibly

Assess fit with our expertise, social benefit, delivery capacity and measurability.

03

Start with a focused project

Agree scope, owner, timing and support before starting. Pro bono or reduced-fee support depends on a capacity review.

04

Measure and learn

Record specialist hours, delivered work, learning and stakeholder feedback. Views and likes alone do not demonstrate social impact.

Dignity and trust come first

Dignity and trust come first

Human dignity, informed consent for images and stories, data protection and honest claims guide our social collaborations.
