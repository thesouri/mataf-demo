Understand the place before choosing a solution

Starting at the coast. Reaching the community.

Local needs come first. On Iran’s northern and southern coasts and in regional collaborations, we define the problem and response with host communities rather than applying one template everywhere.

Mataf contributes communication expertise. Environmental and safety assessments belong with qualified specialists and institutions.

How collaboration works

From a clear need to evidence of change.

01

Listen and understand

Clarify the need, location and audience with the local community.

02

Select responsibly

Assess fit with our expertise, social benefit, delivery capacity and measurability.

03

Deliver a defined pilot

Agree scope, owner, timing and support before starting. Pro bono or reduced-fee support depends on a capacity review.

04

Measure and learn

Record specialist hours, delivered work, learning and stakeholder feedback. Views and likes alone do not demonstrate social impact.
