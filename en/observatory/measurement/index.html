Write the question before building the dashboard

Identify the decision you need to make about acquisition, conversion, lead quality or retention. Then define the data that decision requires.

Look at quality alongside quantity

More enquiries alone do not mean better performance. Audience fit, information quality and the follow-up stage matter too.

Be clear about data limitations

Some audience behaviour may be invisible. Attributing a sale to advertising does not prove advertising caused it. Separate observed data from analytical interpretation in reports.
