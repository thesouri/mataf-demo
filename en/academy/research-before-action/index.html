Separate the request from the problem

“We need a new logo” is a request. The problem might be low recognition, a mistaken impression or inconsistent touchpoints. Before choosing a deliverable, describe what has happened and what evidence shows a need for change.

Make the question serve a decision

Instead of asking broadly what customers want, define the decision ahead: which segment, promise or touchpoint should take priority? Then identify what evidence could change your current choice.

Choose the method after the question

An in-depth conversation can help explore why a behaviour occurs; assessing how widespread a pattern is requires data suited to the target population. Interviews, surveys and data analysis are not interchangeable. Match the method to the question and real constraints.
